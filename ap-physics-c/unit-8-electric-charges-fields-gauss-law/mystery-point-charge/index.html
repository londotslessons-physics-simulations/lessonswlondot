Mystery Point Charge Lab

Determine the magnitude and sign of the unknown charge using electric-field measurements along the x-axis.

Student controls

Instructions

1. Drag the yellow probe only along the x-axis.
2. Record x and the x-component of the field, Ex.
3. Calculate r = |x| and 1/r².
4. Graph Ex vs. 1/r².
5. Use the slope to determine q.

Field model

RelationshipEx = kq/x²
For x > 0, the sign of Ex matches the sign of q. For x < 0, the sign reverses.
