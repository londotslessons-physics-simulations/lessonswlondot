Student Task

  1. Record the beam height.
  2. Choose a clamp separation between 15 cm and 65 cm.
  3. Move the hook until both spring scales match.
  4. Record the hook height and tension.
  5. Repeat for at least five different clamp separations of your choice.
  6. Calculate Δx = separation ÷ 2.
  7. Calculate Δy = beam height − hook height.
  8. Use Δx and Δy to determine the string angle θ for each trial.
  9. Calculate sin θ for each trial.
  10. Use your calculated sin θ to determine the vertical component T sin θ.
  11. Use at least five balanced data points and 2T sin θ = mg to calculate the unknown mass.
  12. Enter your calculated mass and check your answer.
