Newton's Laws — Modified Atwood Machine

Investigate how driving force, system mass, incline angle, and friction affect acceleration.

Experimental Setup

A block on a track is connected by a light string over a pulley to a hanging mass.

Inclined mode: students can set the track anywhere from 0° to 40°.
