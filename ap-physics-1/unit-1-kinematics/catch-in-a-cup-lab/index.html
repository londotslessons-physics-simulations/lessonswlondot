Part 1 Student Task

  1. Select a starting position and marble diameter.
  2. Release the marble through the photogate.
  3. Record the photogate time.
  4. Repeat for three total trials.
  5. For each trial, calculate speed using the marble diameter.
  6. Enter each calculated speed in the table.
  7. Use the average speed for Part 2.
Reminder:
Convert the diameter from centimeters to meters before calculating speed.

Part 2 — Prediction Calculations

Part 2 Student Task

  1. Set the table height.
  2. Calculate the time the marble will be in the air.
  3. Use your Part 1 average launch speed to calculate the horizontal range.
  4. Enter your predicted range and move the cup.
  5. Release the marble.
  6. Compare the actual landing location with your prediction.
Experimental variation:
The real launch in Part 2 includes a small random variation, just like an actual lab. A correct prediction may not land exactly at the cup center.
